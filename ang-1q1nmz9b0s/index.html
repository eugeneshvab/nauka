Grammar 01

Present Simple

For habits, facts and routines. zwyczaje i fakty

I/you/we/they play football.
He/she/it plays football.
She doesn't play.
Does he play?

Clues: always, usually, often, every day
Grammar 02

Present Continuous

For things happening now. co dzieje się teraz

I am studying.
He is wearing a jacket.
They are playing.
She isn't sleeping.

Clues: now, at the moment, Look!, Listen!
Grammar 03

To be / Have got

To be = być, have got = mieć.

I am twelve. She is clever.
They aren't tired.
Are you ready?
I have got blue eyes.
He hasn't got a beard.
Has she got a pet?
Vocabulary 01

People & emotions

Appearance = wygląd, character = charakter, feelings = uczucia.

Appearance: slim, tall, wavy hair, freckles, beard
Character: kind, sociable, stubborn, selfish, confident
Feelings: bored, embarrassed, exhausted, frightened, proud

🎯 Test trick

First find the time clue: every day → Present Simple; now / at the moment → Present Continuous. For he/she/it, add -s in Present Simple, but don't add it after does/doesn't.
